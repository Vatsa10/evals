# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and teams evaluating AI applications with Claude Code, Codex, or the
manual Python SDK. They inspect failures, traces, changes, and regression evidence.

## Product Purpose

Help a person and their coding agent evaluate a real application, implement a
fix, and verify the result against unchanged criteria in their existing workflow.

## Brand Personality

Direct, precise, evidence-led. The established visual direction is monochrome,
with restrained glass surfaces on the marketing site.

## Anti-references

Illustrative scores presented as measured results, missing coverage presented as
success, and decorative UI that obscures the underlying evaluation evidence.

## Design Principles

- Show observed results and their scope together.
- Keep a readable path from a score to its cases and evidence.
- Make setup and comparison useful inside the coding workflow.
- Preserve clear distinctions between live execution, replay, and test doubles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain the existing keyboard, responsive-layout, and reduced-motion behavior.
Use text labels alongside states and readable contrast in report views.
